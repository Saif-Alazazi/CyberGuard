CyberGuard — Privacy Policy
CyberGuard UAE — Scam Message & Link Checker (browser extension & website)

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In one line

CyberGuard only receives the link or message text that you choose to check, uses it solely to tell you whether it looks like a scam, and never sells it or uses it for advertising.

What we receive

  • Links and message text you submit. When you paste a message or check a page and press “Check”, the text (or the page’s web address) is sent to the CyberGuard analysis service so it can be scored.
  • Technical request data. Like any website, our server briefly sees the IP address a request comes from, used only for rate-limiting and abuse prevention.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not read your pages, browsing history, keystrokes or tabs in the background. The extension only acts when you open it and press a button.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, passwords or payment details.

How the browser extension behaves

  • “Check a message” sends only the text you paste into the box.
  • “This page” uses the activeTab permission to read the current tab’s web address only when you click the button, and sends that address to be checked.
  • No data is collected or transmitted unless you actively request a check.

Storage and retention

A checked web address and its verdict may be stored with a reference (“Case ID”) so an evidence record can be produced and so repeat scams can be recognised. These records are not linked to your identity. We keep them only as long as needed for the detection service.

Third parties

Checks run on CyberGuard’s own analysis service. To assess a link, that service may query public security sources (for example domain-registration and certificate records and public threat feeds). Deep checks may use a sandboxed URL-scanning provider to safely open a page on our behalf — your browser never opens the suspicious page.

Children

CyberGuard is a general-audience safety tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change. Material changes will be noted on this page.
