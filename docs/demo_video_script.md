# CyberGuard - 60-second demo video script

Recorded by a team member, in their own voice, while clicking through the **live app**. AI-generated video is not allowed.

## Before you press record

1. Open the live Render URL and wait for the page to load. The free plan sleeps after 15 minutes idle, and the first request can take about a minute.
2. Click **Fake RTA fine** once, so the service is warm and every later scan answers in a few seconds. Then reload the page.
3. Make sure the interface is in **English** (top-right button reads `عربي`) and **Fast** mode is selected.
4. Browser at 100% zoom, full screen, no other tabs visible. Screen recorder: 1080p, microphone on.

## Script

| Time | Click / show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:06 | The home page, cursor still. | "Scam links pretending to be RTA, UAE PASS and UAE banks reach people every day. This is CyberGuard." |

Point at the red **100 - High-risk scam** gauge, then at **Impersonates: RTA Dubai - official domain rta.ae**. | "I paste a real-looking Arabic SMS. CyberGuard finds the link, sees it imitates RTA with a fake gov.ae suffix, and notes the sender is a Philippine number." |

| 0:22-0:27 | Click the **عربي** button. The whole page switches to Arabic, right-to-left. Click it again to return to English. | "The whole interface works in Arabic too." |
| 0:27-0:35 | Scroll up. Click the **bit.ly → real bank** chip. Point at the green **Official domain** badge and the final URL `bankfab.com`. | "A short link is followed to the end. This one lands on First Abu Dhabi Bank, so it is marked official, not flagged." |
| 0:35-0:42 | Click the **Fake RTA fine** chip again. Click **PDF report**. The evidence PDF opens; scroll it once. | "One click gives police-ready evidence: score, reasons, WHOIS, hosting, hashes, UTC and Gulf time, and the Aman report steps." |
| 0:42-0:51 | Close the PDF. Click the **Early warnings** tab. Point at **parkin.vip**: "New domain", first seen 23 Nov, red **not on blocklists yet**. | "We also catch threats before anyone reports them: look-alikes of Parkin, DEWA and Salik registered yesterday, not yet on any blocklist." |
| 0:51-0:57 | Click the **Scam network** tab. Point at the cluster of 8 parcel domains sharing one IP. | "And we link scam domains into campaigns through shared servers." |
| 0:57-1:00 | Cursor still on the graph. | "CyberGuard: explain the scam, prove it, take it down." |

## If something is slow on the day

- If a scan takes more than ~5 seconds, keep talking over the line for that step; do not cut.
- Deep mode (screenshot + AI) takes up to 45 seconds. It is deliberately **not** in the 60-second video; show it live in the 5-minute pitch instead.
