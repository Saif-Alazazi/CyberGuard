# CyberGuard - 60-second demo video (record in your own voice)

Live app: **https://cyberguard-m7tq.onrender.com**. AI-generated video or voice is not allowed: record your own screen and your own voice.

## 1. Before you record (5 minutes)

1. On a laptop, open Chrome and go to https://cyberguard-m7tq.onrender.com. The first load can take up to a minute if the site was asleep.
2. Warm it up: click the **Fake RTA fine (Arabic SMS)** demo button and wait for the result. Then select **Deep (screenshot + AI)**, click **Fake UAE PASS**, and wait until a screenshot appears (about 15 s). Then press F5 to reload the page.
3. Check: the top-right button says **عربي** (the page is in English), and **Fast** is selected.
4. Close other tabs, zoom at 100% (Ctrl+0), full screen (F11).
5. Recorder: Windows **Win+Alt+R** (Xbox Game Bar), or OBS / Loom. Microphone on, 1080p.
6. Practise once without recording. Speak slowly: about 130 words in 60 seconds.

## 3. If something goes wrong (fast fixes)

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Site shows a loading page or takes long | It was asleep. Wait 60 s, reload, warm up again (step 1.2). |
| Deep scan takes longer than 20 s | Keep talking: "it is opening the page in a safe cloud browser". Don't cut. |
| No screenshot appears | Say "the screenshot goes into the evidence pack when the page answers", then click **PDF report** as planned. |
| A demo button gives a different score | Fine. The verdict colour and reasons are what matter. Read the top reason aloud instead. |
| The whole site is down | Record the same flow using the PDF evidence and slides, and say the live site is at the URL. (Unlikely: it passed 46/46 live checks at 03:45.) |
| You go over 60 s | Skip the Arabic switch (0:15-0:20); it is also in the live pitch. |

## 4. Upload the video

- **YouTube:** Create → Upload video → choose the file → visibility **Unlisted** → copy the link.
- **Or Google Drive:** upload → right-click → Share → General access: **Anyone with the link** (Viewer) → Copy link.
- Open the link in a private/incognito window to make sure it plays without logging in.
