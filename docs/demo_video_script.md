# CyberGuard - 60-second demo video (simple version)

You make **two recordings**: (1) the screen, with no talking; (2) your voice on your phone. Then they are combined into one video. It is your real voice, not AI.

Live app: **https://cyberguard-m7tq.onrender.com**

---

## Step 1 - Get ready (3 minutes)

1. On the laptop, open Chrome and go to the live app. Wait until it loads (first time can take 1 minute).
2. Warm it up: click **Fake RTA fine**, wait for the result. Click **Deep**, click **Fake UAE PASS**, wait for the screenshot. Press **F5** to reload.
3. Make sure the page is in **English** and **Fast** is selected.
4. Press **F11** (full screen). Close other tabs.

## Step 2 - Record the screen, no talking (about 60 seconds)

Start recording: press **Win + Alt + R** (press again to stop). Move the mouse slowly. Wait about 2 seconds after each result appears.

1. **(0-5 s)** Show the home page. Do nothing.
2. **(5-15 s)** Click **Fake RTA fine (Arabic SMS)**. Move the mouse over the red **100** and the line **Impersonates: RTA**.
3. **(15-20 s)** Scroll down slowly over the reasons. Click **عربي**, wait 2 seconds, click **English**.
4. **(20-35 s)** Scroll to the top. Click **Deep**. Click **Fake UAE PASS**. Wait until the **screenshot** shows. Point at it.
5. **(35-41 s)** Click **PDF report**. Scroll the PDF once. Close that tab.
6. **(41-47 s)** Click **Fast**. Click **bit.ly → real bank**. Point at the green **Official domain**.
7. **(47-53 s)** Click the **Early warnings** tab. Point at **parkin.vip** and "not on blocklists yet".
8. **(53-60 s)** Click the **Scam network** tab. Point at a group of connected dots.

Stop recording (**Win + Alt + R**). The video is saved in `Videos\Captures`.

## Step 3 - Record your voice on your phone (play the video and talk over it)

Play the screen video on the laptop. Start the phone's **voice recorder**, say **"three, two, one"**, then press play on the laptop and read each line when you see that part:

| When you see... | Say (slowly and clearly) |
|---|---|
| The home page | "Scam links that pretend to be RTA, UAE PASS and banks reach UAE phones every day. This is CyberGuard." |
| The red 100 | "I paste a real scam message in Arabic. The sender can pretend to be RTA, but CyberGuard checks the link and shows it is a fake RTA site. The real one is rta.ae." |
| The reasons and Arabic | "It explains why in English and in Arabic." |
| Deep mode and the screenshot | "Deep mode opens the page safely and takes a screenshot as proof." |
| The PDF | "One click gives the police an evidence report." |
| The green Official | "A short link to a real bank is followed to the end and marked official, not a scam." |
| Early warnings | "It also finds new fake websites the day they are created, before anyone is scammed." |
| Scam network | "And it connects scam websites into one network. CyberGuard: detect, explain, prove." |

About 110 words. If you make a mistake, just record again.

## Step 4 - Send the two files

Put both files in the folder **`C:\Users\Sa940\Documents\CyberGuard\video\`** on the laptop:
- the screen video (from `Videos\Captures`)
- the phone voice recording (send it to the laptop by WhatsApp Web, email to yourself, or Google Drive)

Then say **"files are in the video folder"**. The combined video (your voice + screen, with English subtitles) will be saved as `video\CyberGuard_demo.mp4`, ready to upload.

## Step 5 - Upload

- **YouTube:** Create → Upload → choose `CyberGuard_demo.mp4` → visibility **Unlisted** → copy the link.
- **Or Google Drive:** upload → Share → **Anyone with the link** → copy the link.
- Test the link in an incognito window.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Page is slow the first time | It was asleep. Wait 1 minute and reload. |
| No screenshot in Deep mode | Skip pointing at it; go straight to **PDF report**. |
| Video longer than 60 s | Fine: it will be trimmed when combined. |
| Short on time | Upload the screen video alone (no voice). It still shows everything. |
