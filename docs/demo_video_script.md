# CyberGuard - 60-second demo video script

Recorded by a team member, in their own voice, while clicking through the **live app**. AI-generated video or voice is not allowed. About 130 spoken words.

## Before you press record

1. Open the live app at https://cyberguard-m7tq.onrender.com and wait for the page to load. The free plan sleeps after 15 minutes idle, and the first request can take about a minute.
2. Warm it up: click **Fake RTA fine**, then select **Deep**, click **Fake UAE PASS** and wait for the screenshot to appear. Then reload the page.
3. Interface in **English** (top-right button reads `عربي`), **Fast** mode selected.
4. Browser at 100% zoom, full screen, no other tabs visible. Recorder at 1080p, microphone on.

## If something is slow on the day

- If the deep scan takes longer than ~15 s, keep describing what it checks; do not cut.
- If the screenshot does not appear (Cloudflare busy), the page still shows the reasons. Say "the screenshot is in the evidence PDF when available" and move on.
