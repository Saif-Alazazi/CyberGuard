**Project Title:**
CyberGuard - UAE scam-link intelligence

**Brief Description** (551 characters, limit 600):

CyberGuard checks a link or a whole SMS/WhatsApp message for UAE-targeted scams. It follows redirects and shorteners, checks the final site against 47 verified UAE bank and government entities, detects look-alikes in English and Arabic, and queries threat feeds and Google Safe Browsing, then gives a 0-100 risk score with reasons in English and Arabic. It exports police evidence (PDF, JSON, STIX 2.1), flags newly registered UAE look-alike domains, links scam domains into campaigns, and its Safe Links gateway checks each tapped link at click time.
